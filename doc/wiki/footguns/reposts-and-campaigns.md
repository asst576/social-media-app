# Reposts and Campaign Delivery

- A repost is a new `Post` referencing its original, not a copied post. A database constraint limits each member to one repost of an original. `PROTECT` prevents deleting an original while reposts refer to it.
- Campaigns are not posts. Keep ads out of the organic post query; campaign eligibility and sponsored labeling are separate.
- Staff approval alone does not make an ad deliverable. The campaign must also be simulated-paid and within its schedule.
- Simulated purchases do not transfer money or consume the budget per impression. Do not describe demo budget as actual spend or guaranteed delivery.
